Safety comes first. Always.

  • Every system uses inverters with anti-islanding protection, so your system shuts off safely when the grid goes down and cannot hurt line workers.
  • Your system is installed by a SAPVIA PV GreenCard holder, the solar industry's mark of trained, safe installers.
  • A certified electrician checks and signs off every system, and you receive your Certificate of Compliance (CoC) before we hand over.
  • Wiring, earthing and surge protection follow South African electrical rules.
  • Panels are fixed so they stay put in strong coastal wind, and our cleaners use harnesses on roofs.
